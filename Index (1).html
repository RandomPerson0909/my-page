A short letter,
long overdue

Dear you,

Do you remember when we first met? Just another day at tuition — same benches, same chapters, and someone new walked in. For everyone else it was ordinary. For me, it was the day the room changed. What I felt for you started as a small crush I could hide behind my notebook. Two years later, it refuses to fit behind anything.

I have tried to tell you so many times. I rehearsed it on the way to class, perfected it through the lesson, and lost all of it the moment you smiled. "Tomorrow," I told myself. Every tomorrow. For more than two years.

I'm done waiting for a braver version of me to show up — he's not coming. So today, the one who waited two years built you a whole website instead. Because apparently, that's who I am now.

— the one who was always a little too quiet around you
